\begin{abstract}
Balade is a browser extension that places autonomous animated cursors on web
pages. They wander, inspect elements, type ghost text, greet each other, and
anticipate the user. No neural network is involved: every behavior is a
coroutine (a generator used as a state machine) driven by classical
mathematics --- Fitts's law, minimum-jerk profiles, arc geometry, damped
springs, Reynolds steering, Markov chains, and Bayesian-style scoring.
This paper documents the formulas, the engineering trade-offs, and the
benchmarks that replaced naive implementations with faster exact ones.
\end{abstract}

\section{Behaviors as coroutines}
Each cursor runs a generator: \texttt{*run(cursor)} yields the frame time step
and resumes. The engine pumps all cursors from a single requestAnimationFrame
loop with one shared clock, so reads and writes stay batched and the
simulation time is coherent. Composition uses \texttt{yield*}:
\texttt{moveTo}, \texttt{wait}, \texttt{click} are reusable subroutines.

\section{Movement mathematics}

\subsection{Duration: Fitts's law}
\begin{equation}
T = \frac{a + b \log_2\left(1 + D/W\right)}{v}, \quad \text{clamped to } [T_{\min}, T_{\max}]
\end{equation}
$D$ is the distance, $W$ the target width, $v$ a speed multiplier.

\subsection{Profile: minimum jerk with time warp}
\begin{equation}
s(t) = 10t^3 - 15t^4 + 6t^5, \qquad u = s(t^q), \; q \in [0.68, 0.85]
\end{equation}
Each cursor keeps its own warp $q$ for life: a recognizable motor signature.

\subsection{Paths: turtle arcs and similarity}
Paths are traced turtle-style along circular arcs of constant curvature, then
mapped by a similarity (rotation, scale, translation) onto the target.

\subsection{Settling: semi-implicit Euler spring}
\begin{equation}
a = -k\,x - c\,v
\end{equation}

\subsection{Swarm: Reynolds steering}
Orbit with breathing radius, 3-lobe rosace, and a swirl factor.

\section{Measured optimizations}
Iterative heading rotation ($4\times$), sampled progress curves (zero
\texttt{pow} per frame), triple-angle expansion without \texttt{atan2}
($3\times$), squared-distance scans ($2\times$). Rejected after measurement:
sine tables ($3\times$ slower), order-5 Taylor (wrong), cosine pre-filters.

\section{Simulated cognition}
First-order Markov habits, satiety with exponential decay, novelty pressure,
gesture anticipation, social roles (leader, follower, loner), and moods.

\section{Conclusion}
Lifelike behavior needs no learning: composable state machines plus the
right classical formulas are enough.
